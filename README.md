# Hadal Descent

A deep-sea survival roguelite in a single HTML file. Sink from the twilight zone (200 m) to the Challenger Deep (10,935 m). Your weapons fire on their own. You swim, collect glowing plankton, and pick an upgrade each time you level.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any modern browser. Nothing to install or build.

## Controls
- **Swim:** WASD or arrow keys. On touch, drag anywhere on the screen.
- **Pick an upgrade:** click it, or press 1, 2 or 3. **R** rerolls.
- **Pause:** P or Esc.

## What makes each run different
- **5 weapons and 9 traits.** You can hold up to 4 weapons and 5 traits, so every build is a trade-off.
- **4 depth zones**, each with its own creatures and a boss: Giant Squid, Gulper King, Lanternmaw, and the Hadal Leviathan.
- **Swarm events, rare drops** (kelp heals, currents pull in all plankton, pearls), and critical hits.
- **Permanent upgrades** bought with pearls between dives.
- **3 creatures** to unlock. Lumen the anglerfish is available from the start. Medusa the jelly unlocks at 1,000 m and Volt the torpedo ray at 4,000 m.
- **Endless mode** after you beat the final boss.

Progress is saved in your browser's localStorage.
